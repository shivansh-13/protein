When I became President of the Computer Science Association (CSA) at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, I learned that inspiring a team and making an organisation pay for itself are different skills. As Joint Secretary I coordinated core tech and design teams across 50+ events; as President I led the Fest Organising Body for Atmos '22, where our teams generated INR 4.6 lakh in revenue, and CSA won the Best Association Award that year. Coming from a small town, this was the first time I saw that I could earn people's trust and turn it into results.

My Practice School internship at Dr. Reddy's Laboratories showed me where business and data meet. On the Emerging Markets Finance Transformation, I worked with 10+ business units to define and validate 200+ Material Master data-quality rules, then translated them into controls across SAP, Informatica and GCP with engineers and product teams. The hard part was not the systems; it was getting finance, supply chain and IT to agree on what "correct data" meant.

At Fareportal I own the flight search and filtering services used by 600K+ people daily. I conceived and shipped AI-powered natural-language flight filters, turning free-text queries into structured searches with Gemini and later Vertex AI, and built an LLM-powered pull-request reviewer for our engineers.

Neither is live today. The filters ran in production, but our Associate Director of Product later removed them because I could not show that they improved conversion. The PR reviewer was dropped when the company chose a commercial tool, because I had no business case to show ours was the better investment. Both worked technically; both failed because I could not prove their value. That gap, deciding which AI features are worth building, measuring their impact, and convincing leadership, is what motivates this application.

My objective in joining IIM Ahmedabad's Blended PGP in Business Analytics and AI is to close it. The Analytics Project Lifecycle course would give me what my filters lacked: a way to define success metrics and build the business case before writing code. The action-learning projects would let me apply this to real problems at Fareportal while I continue to own products used by hundreds of thousands of people. A cohort of experienced professionals from different industries will also test my thinking far beyond what a single company can.

My aspirations follow from this. In the short term, I aim to move into a product manager role at Fareportal, owning outcomes, not just code. In the long term, I want to become Head of AI and Product at a travel or consumer-tech company, deciding which AI bets are worth making and proving that they pay off.
